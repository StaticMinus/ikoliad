## 🧬 Design DNA Protocol & Workspace Standards

### Dimension 1: Design System (Measurable Tokens)
- **Palette**:
  - `surface.background`: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) / Soft Cloud Gray (`#FBFBFD` / `#F5F5F7`)
  - `primary.hex`: Semantic Apple Blue (`#0071E3`)
  - `secondary.hex`: Deep Charcoal (`#1D1D1F`)

- **Typography Hierarchy**:

  - `heading`: `font-bold text-xl sm:text-2xl md:text-3xl tracking-tight text-[#1D1D1F]`
  - `body`: `font-normal text-sm sm:text-base text-gray-500 leading-relaxed`
  - `eyebrow / mono`: `font-mono text-[10px] sm:text-[11px] font-bold uppercase tracking-wider`
- **Bento Geometry & Shape**:
  - `border_radius`: Cards (`rounded-[32px]`), Modals (`rounded-[32px]`), Buttons & Pills (`rounded-full`), Sub-modules (`rounded-2xl`)
